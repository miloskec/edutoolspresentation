Investor briefing

EduToolsFactory - AI-native assessment & survey intelligence for organizations

EduToolsFactory unifies MCQs, surveys, analytics, document extraction and AI-driven test generation into a single, secure platform for schools, companies, training centers and research organizations.

  • AI-powered generation of MCQ tests and surveys from real documents, URLs, or uploaded files.
  • Digital-first engine that can also ingest paper-based tests.
  • Both cloud SaaS and fully offline, on-premise variants for sensitive environments.

Early-stage product with a working MCQ & survey engine, live URL→MCQ workflows, and an active roadmap for enterprise and education adoption.

Current product and technical maturity

Mature MCQ engine (already live)

  • URL-based MCQ generation workflows.
  • Manual MCQ creation with rich text, math, images and inline previews.
  • Full MCQ object: question + 4 options (1 correct, 3 distractors) + explanations + “why others are wrong” + references.
  • Reordering, draft mode, versioning, imports and AI-assisted generation.

Survey engine with flexible question types

  • Single choice, multiple choice, scale and free text questions.
  • Unlimited number of choices per question (unlike MCQs).
  • Configurable scoring and legends for final interpretations.
  • Designed for HR, training feedback, pulse checks and research studies.

Behind the scenes, the survey engine already supports dimension-based scoring and profile-style outputs (e.g. stress & coping styles, engagement factors, personality-like profiles). In the current release, surveys are deliberately positioned in a “light” non-clinical mode for self-reflection, HR/L&D insight and research, even though the underlying architecture can support more advanced use cases. Read more about the survey & profiling roadmap

Remote API for integration

  • Token-protected API endpoints with strict authentication.
  • Application-level request firewall (OWASP-style patterns).
  • Per-user + per-IP rate limiting with HTTP 429 enforcement.
  • Compatible with LMS platforms, HR systems and internal portals.

SaaS Pricing Model (Planned)

  • Free tier – limited MCQs and survey responses; ideal for individuals.
  • Pro tier – unlimited tests, higher quotas, bundled AI tokens + overage-based billing.
  • Institution tier – multi-teacher licensing, central analytics, SSO, on-prem options.
  • API tier – pay-per-token or per-generation for third-party integrations.

Pricing tiers and usage rules may be optimized in collaboration with investors as the market shifts - the platform's modular SaaS design allows rapid adaptation to new opportunities, customer behavior and technological change.
