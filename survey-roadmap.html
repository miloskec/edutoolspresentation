Roadmap briefing

Survey & Profiling Roadmap

EduToolsFactory already ships a dimension-based survey engine and is building toward more advanced, regulated use cases with qualified partners.

Talk to the team Back to investor briefing

Current status – "light mode" survey engine

The survey engine supports Likert-style questions, multiple dimensions per survey (e.g., stress & coping styles, engagement, personality-like profiles), weighted contributions of each answer option to one or more dimensions, and reverse-scored items plus control questions.

Outputs are profile-like summaries with dimension scores and concise interpretations, intended for self-reflection, team and organizational insight (HR/L&D), and research - style instruments. Results are intended for self-reflection, team and organizational insight, and research - not for diagnosis or automated clinical decisions.

This is a deliberate "light mode" positioning: it is not a clinical or diagnostic tool, does not assign clinical labels, and is designed for non-clinical contexts.

Phase 1 – Professional-grade non-clinical surveys (HR, L&D, research)

Product hardening

  • Richer survey templates (engagement, burnout risk, training feedback, pulse surveys).
  • Improved dimension legends and profile summaries.
  • Entity-level analytics across teams, departments and cohorts.
  • Exportable reports (PDF/CSV) for HR leaders and educators.

Governance & safety

  • Clear non-clinical disclaimers across instruments and reports.
  • Configuration that prevents accidental clinical wording.
  • Audit logs for who created and changed instruments.

Phase 1 keeps surveys firmly in the non-clinical domain but makes them production-ready for organizations.

Phase 2 – Clinical pre-screening (pre-filter for professionals)

Pursued only with qualified partners and under regulatory guidance. EduToolsFactory would act as a pre-screening layer that flags elevated risk or "needs professional review", but never replaces clinical judgment.

  • Validated instruments and protocols: Standard, validated questionnaires for specific populations with documented reliability and validity.
  • Normative data and evidence-based cut-offs: Norm datasets (percentiles, T-scores, thresholds) instead of heuristic rules.
  • Governance and professional oversight: Licensed psychologists/psychiatrists and ethics boards defining instruments, labels and follow-up paths.
  • Consent and user experience for sensitive data: Clear informed-consent flows and explicit explanations of what is measured and how results will be used.
  • Platform safeguards: Role-based access so only authorized professionals see sensitive summaries; end-users see simplified, non-alarming language.

Phase 3 – Decision-support tools for professionals (optional, long-term)

An optional, longer-term path that depends on domain partnerships, regulatory feasibility and investor appetite for a clinical vertical.

  • Instrument catalog and norm libraries maintained with clinical partners.
  • Clinician-focused dashboards with drill-down, history and professional reports.
  • Regulatory alignment with applicable medical-device and local assessment laws.
  • Strict separation of roles: EduToolsFactory as a structured data and workflow layer; decisions remain with licensed professionals.

Cross-cutting technical enablers

  • Role-based access control and tenant isolation for sensitive surveys.
  • Encryption in transit and at rest for assessment data.
  • Detailed audit logs for instrument creation, edits and profile views.
  • Data-retention policies and EU-friendly data residency options.
  • Offline/on-prem deployments for institutions with strict data policies.

Investor takeaway

The survey engine is fully functional today and actively usable in "light mode" for HR, education, training and general research. Even in its current form, the platform reliably supports multi-dimension scoring, legends, domain-specific interpretations and flexible questionnaire structures - making it immediately deployable in real-world environments.

At the same time, the underlying architecture was intentionally designed for upward scalability. With the right expert partners and structured feedback loops, the existing foundation can evolve into significantly more advanced capabilities, including:

  • Regulated pre-screening workflows
    (e.g. automated early-risk assessment for burnout, early stress indicators in employees, or candidate pre-screening before interviews based on validated protocols)
  • Decision-support models requiring clinical or psychological oversight
    (e.g. models that suggest to HR "candidates with this profile have a higher probability of success in this role", or tools for coaches/counselors that highlight potential learning difficulties, adaptation issues, or team-dynamics risks)
  • Validated scoring norms and population-specific benchmarks
    (e.g. comparing a person's results with norms for the EU, a specific region, industry, or age group-similar to professional psychometric tests that provide percentiles, not just raw scores)
  • Reliability/consistency metrics and longitudinal tracking
    (e.g. tracking an employee's progress over several months, detecting drops in performance or increases in stress, checking whether responses remain stable over time-used in corporate wellness and development programs)

The roadmap clearly separates what is already delivered and usable today from features that would require formal medical/psychological governance to meet regulatory or ethical standards. This ensures that growth is both responsible and strategically aligned - while preserving a very large opportunity space for future expansion built on the strong technical base that already exists.
