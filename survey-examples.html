EduToolsFactory Survey Library (Demo Inventories)

All instruments below are non-clinical demo inventories designed for coaching, HR, education, and wellbeing programs. They use the same survey engine: multi-dimensional scoring, profile bands, and rich interpretations. Scores are heuristic indicators, not diagnoses or performance labels, and should always be combined with context and professional judgement.

Personality & Behavioral Style

Big Five – OCEAN (50 items)

What it screens:
Classic Big Five traits – Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Emotional Stability.

Use cases:
Personal development, coaching intake, team workshops, educational programs.

Structure:
50 Likert items mapped to the five OCEAN dimensions, with profile bands (e.g. lower / balanced / higher) per trait.

Notes:
Loosely inspired by Big Five research; configured as a lightweight, non-clinical implementation.

LIVE SURVEY -> Big Five – OCEAN

Adaptive Behavioral Style Inventory (ABSI)

What it screens:
Everyday behavioral style across five dimensions:
Emotional Regulation, Social Engagement, Goal Orientation & Discipline, Cognitive Flexibility, Interpersonal Sensitivity.

Use cases:
Coaching, leadership development, HR talent programs, onboarding assessments.

Structure:
Likert items with reverse-keyed questions and three profile bands per dimension (low / balanced / high).

Positioning:
Focuses on how people tend to respond and adapt, not on fixed personality labels.

LIVE SURVEY -> ABSI

Stress & Coping Styles Inventory

What it screens:
Preferred coping styles under pressure: Emotional Stability vs Reactivity, Sociability/Extraversion, Seeking Support, Avoidance/Disengagement.

Use cases:
Manager/employee coaching, wellbeing programs, resilience workshops.

Structure:
Scenario-based single-choice items where each option contributes to one or more coping dimensions.

Positioning:
Designed to support conversations about coping strategies; not a mental-health diagnostic.

LIVE SURVEY -> Stress & Coping Styles

Lifestyle & Wellbeing (New Demo Cluster)

These four instruments extend the same architecture into everyday wellbeing and lifestyle habits.

All are explicitly:

  • non-clinical,
  • inspired by public-health guidelines and coaching practice,
  • implemented as flexible demo inventories for HR, wellbeing programs, and SaaS integrations.

Everyday Focus, Energy & Digital Balance (Demo)

Dimensions (examples):
Deep Focus & Attention Quality, Intentional vs Reactive Digital Routines, Notification & Multitask Load, Energy & Recovery Rhythm, Boundaries for Screen-Free Rest.

Goal:
Offer a non-clinical snapshot of how intentionally people manage their focus, energy, and digital ecosystems so wellbeing teams can spark reflective conversations rather than diagnoses.

Use cases:
Wellbeing coaching, digital wellbeing initiatives, energy-management workshops, SaaS demo content.

Profiles:
Narrative bands per dimension (e.g. deliberate focus architect vs reactive multitasker, steady vs depleted recovery) with reminders that insights are illustrative, not medical advice.

LIVE SURVEY -> Everyday Focus, Energy & Digital Balance

Physical Activity & Lifestyle Fitness Screener (Demo)

Dimensions (examples):
Weekly Movement Frequency, Activity Intensity, Sedentary Time, Recovery & Injury Prevention, Motivation & Consistency.

Goal:
Provide a quick snapshot of how closely everyday movement patterns approximate general recommendations (e.g. WHO physical-activity guidelines) without turning into a diagnostic tool.

Use cases:
Corporate wellbeing programs, fitness/wellness apps, lifestyle onboarding flows.

Profiles:
Per-dimension bands (e.g. lower / balanced / higher movement, higher vs lower sedentary load) to support coaching conversations.

LIVE SURVEY -> Physical Activity & Lifestyle Fitness

Healthy Eating & Nutrition Habits Inventory (Demo)

Dimensions (examples):
Whole-Foods Emphasis, Processed & Convenience Foods Load, Meal Regularity & Rhythm, Hydration Balance, Mindful vs Automatic Eating.

Goal:
Map everyday nutrition habits—not diets or medical nutrition—into interpretable patterns.

Use cases:
Wellness programs, lifestyle apps, coaching intake, education about energy and focus.

Profiles:
Bands for each dimension highlighting patterns such as frequent ultra-processed intake, irregular meals, or strong whole-foods emphasis.

LIVE SURVEY -> Healthy Eating & Nutrition Habits

Lifestyle Balance & Recovery Check (Demo)

Dimensions (examples):
Workload & Demand Pressure, Rest & Recovery Quality, Social Support & Connection, Personal Growth & Hobbies, Boundaries & Switch-Off Ability.

Goal:
Capture how balanced someone’s life currently feels between work, rest, relationships, and personal growth.

Use cases:
HR wellbeing programs, leadership/coaching sessions, pre-screen before deeper burnout or mood surveys.

Profiles:
Per-dimension bands (e.g. high demand pressure, strained recovery, strong social base, robust boundaries).

LIVE SURVEY -> Lifestyle Balance & Recovery

Digital Habits & Screen Balance Screener (Demo)

Dimensions (examples):
Screen Time Load, Digital Multitasking Intensity, Evening Screen & Sleep Impact, Focus & Distraction Balance, Sense of Control over Digital Use.

Goal:
Provide a structured view of digital habits that influence focus, sleep and wellbeing.

Use cases:
Digital wellbeing initiatives, parental guidance tools (for adults), productivity and focus apps.

Profiles:
Bands such as high vs balanced screen load, strong vs strained focus, high vs low sense of control.

LIVE SURVEY -> Digital Habits & Screen Balance

Work, Team & Culture

Workplace Engagement & Burnout Risk Pulse

What it screens:
Early signals around engagement, energy, and burnout risk (workload, control, meaning, support, recovery).

Use cases:
Team health checks, periodic “pulse” surveys, leadership dashboards.

Structure:
Short multi-dimensional screener with risk bands rather than hard thresholds.

Positioning:
Framed as a signal for follow-up, not a medical burnout scale.

LIVE SURVEY -> Engagement & Burnout Risk Pulse

Team Collaboration & Role Style Inventory

What it screens:
How people typically contribute in teams: task focus, relationship focus, initiative, structure vs flexibility, conflict handling.

Use cases:
Team workshops, project kick-offs, role clarity discussions.

Structure:
Likert items across several collaboration/role dimensions with narrative profiles per dimension.

Positioning:
Helps teams name complementary styles without implying “good vs bad” roles.

LIVE SURVEY -> Team Collaboration & Role Style

Leadership Focus & Risk Style Inventory (LFRS)

What it screens:
Leadership emphasis (people vs delivery vs innovation), comfort with risk, and approach to governance and guardrails.

Use cases:
Leadership development, succession planning, coaching programs.

Structure:
Multi-dimensional Likert inventory with profile bands (e.g. delivery-heavy vs people-centric vs innovation-centric emphases).

Positioning:
Non-clinical, targeted at leadership style and portfolio of bets, not competence or diagnostic labels.

LIVE SURVEY -> Leadership Focus & Risk Style

Social Safety & Inclusion Climate (Demo)

What it screens:
Perceived psychological safety, inclusion, voice, and fairness in a team or organization.

Use cases:
DEI initiatives, team retrospectives, climate checks.

Structure:
Participant-level survey that can be aggregated to team/unit for climate heatmaps; dimensions such as safety to speak up, belonging, fairness, and respectful behaviour.

Positioning:
Signals areas for follow-up; not a legal or compliance determination by itself.

LIVE SURVEY -> Social Safety & Inclusion Climate

Values & Decision Boundaries Inventory (Demo)

What it screens:
How people navigate rules, principles, gray zones, long-term impact, and speaking up: Rule Orientation, Principle-Driven Integrity, Gray-Zone Tolerance, Long-Term Responsibility, Willingness to Raise Concerns.

Use cases:
Ethics & compliance workshops, leadership programs, culture diagnostics.

Structure:
Likert items with three illustrative bands per dimension (e.g. Flexible Boundary Explorer, Contextual Rule Balancer, Strict Compliance Guardian).

Positioning:
Explicitly marked as demo and non-legal; loosely inspired by ethics/compliance research themes, not by any proprietary instrument.

LIVE SURVEY -> Values & Decision Boundaries

Cognitive, Mood & Recovery Screeners (Demo)

Mood & Daily Resilience Screener (Demo)

What it screens:
Everyday mood tone, stress reactivity, day-to-day resilience, and perceived support.

Use cases:
Wellbeing programs, coaching check-ins, pre-/post-intervention tracking.

Positioning:
Designed as an early-signal screener only. It cannot diagnose depression or anxiety and should never replace professional clinical assessment.

LIVE SURVEY -> Mood & Daily Resilience Screener

Lifestyle Sleep & Fatigue Risk Screener (Demo)

What it screens:
Sleep duration and regularity, sleep hygiene habits, daytime fatigue, and recovery behaviours.

Use cases:
Fatigue risk management, wellbeing campaigns, health coaching.

Positioning:
Non-clinical; highlights patterns that may warrant discussion with a healthcare professional.

LIVE SURVEY -> Lifestyle Sleep & Fatigue Risk Screener

Cognitive Self-Awareness Screener (Demo)

What it screens:
Self-reported attention, memory slips, mental load, and subjective cognitive clarity.

Use cases:
Digital wellbeing programs, workload/burnout conversations, coaching.

Positioning:
Not a tool for diagnosing cognitive decline or neurological conditions. It simply surfaces self-perceived patterns that may deserve further exploration with qualified professionals.

LIVE SURVEY -> Cognitive Self-Awareness Screener
